# Knowledge source attribution

The sample FAR and DFARS records are short excerpts from U.S. Government sources
published at [Acquisition.gov](https://www.acquisition.gov/). They are included
for demonstration and testing, not as a substitute for the current official text.
The DFARS 204.7304(c) sample was retrieved on September 4, 2026 from its
[official section page](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/204.7304-solicitation-provision-and-contract-clauses.);
the page identifies DFARS Change 5/7/2026, effective May 7, 2026. Its manifest
entry preserves the normalized-text checksum and citation ID.

The Smart Matrix adapter targets the Provision and Clause Matrix distributed by
Acquisition.gov/Defense Acquisition University. No Smart Matrix data is vendored.

Every normalized record preserves publisher authority, canonical URL (when
available), retrieval date, source locator, and a deterministic citation ID.
See `manifests/sources.yaml` for the machine-readable source inventory.

# External legal dataset attribution

## MAUD v1

The Merger Agreement Understanding Dataset (MAUD) v1 is published by The
Atticus Project under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
license.

- Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7500064
- Project: https://www.atticusprojectai.org/maud/
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Citation: Wang et al., "MAUD: An Expert-Annotated Legal NLP Dataset for
  Merger Agreement Understanding," EMNLP 2023.

## ContractNLI

ContractNLI is published by Yuta Koreeda and Christopher D. Manning under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.

- Dataset: https://stanfordnlp.github.io/contract-nli/
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Citation: Koreeda and Manning, "ContractNLI: A Dataset for Document-level
  Natural Language Inference for Contracts," Findings of EMNLP 2021.

The prepared training artifacts record immutable source revisions and SHA-256
digests. Publisher development and test labels are excluded from preparation
and training.
